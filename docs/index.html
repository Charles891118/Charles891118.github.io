Welcome!

I am a Master of Social Work (MSW) student at the Brown School at Washington University in St. Louis. Before joining WashU, I completed a Master of Arts in Economics at National Taiwan University, where I also earned a Bachelor of Arts with a double major in Social Work and Economics. This interdisciplinary training has given me a distinctive ability to connect human-centered perspectives from social work with quantitative analysis from economics. My field interest is services for older adults, and my research interests focus on program evaluation and aging issues. Looking ahead, I hope to apply this integrated approach in further academic study and research that contributes to better policies and services for older adults.
